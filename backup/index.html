HOW IT WORKS

Thinks like a fraud investigator.

Not a keyword alarm. SCAMSTOP builds confidence as red flags stack up.

1

Who is contacting you?

Known vs unknown, new number, spoofed contact.

2

Who are they pretending to be?

Your bank, courier, govt, family, employer.

3

What emotion are they triggering?

Fear, urgency, love, greed — to rush you.

4

What action do they want?

Pay now, share OTP, click a link, install app.

5

Where are they redirecting you?

External link, QR, fake site, new UPI ID.

6

What happens after you interact?

Money leaves, data leaks, access gained.

Confidence stacks up

One flag is not enough to warn you. SCAMSTOP waits until several independent signals line up — just like a trained investigator — so you get fewer false alarms and fewer missed scams. Every warning shows its reasons.
